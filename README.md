# DOS Vision Portal

Create a premium, modern, highly professional corporate website for DOS Group, an architecture-led group of companies based in Bangladesh.

Brand Identity

The parent/primary brand is:

DOS — Architecture

DOS should be positioned as a modern architecture, design, planning, and development-focused company with a strong vision for creating meaningful spaces and long-term value.

The website should communicate:

Architectural excellence

Modern design

Trust and professionalism

Innovation

Sustainability

Real estate and development expertise

Strong business ecosystem

Premium Bangladeshi corporate identity

The overall design should feel like a combination of a premium architecture studio + real estate development group + modern corporate group.

VISUAL DESIGN

The website should look premium, minimal, architectural, and international.

Design inspiration:

Foster + Partners

BIG

SOM

Aesop

high-end real estate brands

premium architecture studios

Do NOT copy any specific website. Take inspiration only from their design principles.

Color palette:

Off-white / warm white

Charcoal / near-black

Concrete grey

Subtle muted beige

Very limited accent color

Typography:

Use a sophisticated modern sans-serif

Large editorial headings

Strong typography hierarchy

Generous whitespace

Clean body text

Avoid:

Cheap-looking gradients

Excessive rounded cards

Generic corporate templates

Too many colors

Cluttered layouts

Excessive animations

Stock-photo-heavy appearance

IMPORTANT BRAND DIRECTION

DOS Architecture should remain the visual and strategic center of the website.

The Sister Concerns should support the main DOS brand rather than overpower it.

The website should immediately communicate:

DOS = Architecture + Development + Solutions + Business Ecosystem

The final experience should feel like a serious, established, premium Bangladeshi group of companies, suitable for clients, investors, partners, architects, developers, and corporate stakeholders.

Make the website visually impressive enough that a visitor understands the brand within the first 5 seconds.

Start by building the complete homepage with polished responsive UI, then create the supporting pages and reusable components.
